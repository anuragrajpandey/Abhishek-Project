What you can report

Electricity

  • Frequent power cuts
  • Faulty or exposed wiring
  • Non-working lights
  • AC / fans not working

Wi-Fi

  • Slow internet speed
  • Frequent disconnection
  • No network coverage in hostel or labs

Infrastructure

  • Broken furniture (benches, chairs, tables)
  • Damaged doors or windows
  • Non-functional projectors / ACs
  • Water leakage or cracks in the building

Classroom

  • Overcrowded classrooms
  • Poor lighting or ventilation
  • Dirty greenboard
  • Unclean washrooms
  • Garbage not collected regularly

Faculty-related

  • Syllabus not completed on time
  • Frequent absence without a substitute
  • Unfair evaluation or grading
  • Student doubts not addressed

How it works

  1. Register and log in.
  2. Pick a category and describe the issue.
  3. Admin reviews and updates the status.
  4. Track progress from your account.

Why it matters

  • No lost or delayed paper complaints
  • Clear status and response time
  • One admin dashboard for every complaint
  • A complete record of past complaints
